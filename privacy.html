Privacy Policy - Hermes Workspace

Hermes Workspace is a personal-use application operated by and for a single user (its owner). It accesses only the owner's own Google account data (Google Tasks, Google Calendar availability and events the owner confirms, Gmail message headers and drafts) to organize the owner's own work.

Data is processed on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train models. No e-mail is sent automatically; drafts are only created on request. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Politica de privacidade: aplicativo de uso pessoal, de um unico usuario, que acessa apenas os dados da propria conta Google do dono para organizar as proprias tarefas, agenda e rascunhos de e-mail. Os dados nao sao vendidos, compartilhados nem usados para publicidade.
