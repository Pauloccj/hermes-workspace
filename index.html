Hermes Workspace

Hermes Workspace is a personal-use assistant used by a single person to organize their own tasks, calendar and e-mail drafts through Google APIs. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Hermes Workspace e um assistente de uso pessoal, usado por uma unica pessoa para organizar as proprias tarefas, agenda e rascunhos de e-mail. Nao e oferecido ao publico.
